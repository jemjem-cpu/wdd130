Our Whitewater Trips

Scenic Family Float

Drift through calm turquoise water and gentle Class I-II riffles while your guide shares local wildlife and river stories. Perfect for families with young children and first-time rafters who want views over thrills.

Duration: 2 hours | Difficulty: Easy | Price: $45 per person

Canyon Run

Paddle as a team through a rocky canyon with lively Class II-III rapids and calm pools for swimming. A great balance of excitement and relaxation for groups, friends and beginners ready for a challenge.

Duration: 3 hours | Difficulty: Moderate | Price: $65 per person

Classic Whitewater Adventure

Our most popular trip. Tackle continuous Class III rapids with a full safety briefing, an expert guide in every raft and a riverside lunch included. Ideal for active adventurers who want a full day on the water.

Duration: Half day (4 hours) | Difficulty: Intermediate | Price: $85 per person

Extreme Rapids Expedition

Our most intense trip. Charge through big waves and powerful Class IV-V rapids that demand teamwork and experience. Previous rafting experience and good swimming ability are required.

Duration: Full day (6 hours) | Difficulty: Advanced | Price: $120 per person
